Type a few numbers to make a bill. Commission for every employee, customer credit, expenses, reports and printing, fully offline.

Made for the counter

Bill in seconds

Who sold it, what it was, how much: 1 Enter 2 Enter 1450 Enter. A big number pad on phones.

Employee commission

Rates, targets and bonus; worked out from the bills and paid with a slip. Returns reduce it fairly.

Credit (udhaar)

Know who owes what and since when. Take old dues while billing, send reminders on WhatsApp.

Expenses and reports

Daily cash after expenses, sales by employee and item type, GST, downloads in Excel, PDF and CSV.

Works offline, stays private

No internet needed. Your data is encrypted on the device, with password-protected backups.

Six languages

English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu, in the app and on the printed bill.

Windows and Android
