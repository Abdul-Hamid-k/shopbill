Questions

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to register and start the free trial, and for the occasional licence check. Making bills never needs it.

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer or phone, in an encrypted database. Bills, customers and prices are never sent to anyone. Backups are locked with a password you choose; a forgotten backup password cannot be recovered, so write it down.

Does it file my GST or income tax?

No. It keeps the records and prepares the tables and files your accountant asks for. The accountant files the return.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows computers and Android phones and tablets.

Which printers?

Bills are laid out for 58 mm and 80 mm rolls and for A4, and are printed through the printer you choose. We are testing on real printers: please tell us how it prints on yours.

Which languages?

English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu, in the app and on the printed bill.
