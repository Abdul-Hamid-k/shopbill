Features

Everything here is in the app today

Real screens with made-up shop data (Lakshmi Textiles). Nothing on this page is a mock-up.

Billing

Type 1 Enter 2 Enter 1450 Enter: seller, item type, price. Add more items the same way.

  • Up to six bills open at once, and a bill can be put on hold.
  • Pay by cash, UPI, card, split between methods, or on credit.
  • Discount, customer name and phone, and the change to give back.
  • Save and print, or save only. Bills are laid out for 58 mm and 80 mm rolls and for A4.
  • Send a bill on WhatsApp from the device.

Today, bills and reports

  • Today: sales so far, cash, UPI, card and credit, top seller, commission, money spent and cash after expenses.
  • Bills: find any bill, open it, print it again, take a return.
  • Reports: net sales, expenses and money kept; sales by employee and by item type; GST tables for your accountant.
  • Download reports as Excel, PDF or CSV.

ShopBill keeps the records and prepares the tables. It does not file GST or income tax: your accountant does that.

Customers and credit

  • A list of customers with what each owes and the date of the last bill.
  • Filter to the ones who owe money, or the late ones.
  • Record a payment against old dues, and take dues while billing.

Staff, commission and attendance

  • Commission worked out from the bills, per employee, with rates, targets and bonus.
  • A category can have its own rule (for example sarees).
  • Returns reduce commission; a payment slip can be printed.
  • Mark attendance on its own page.

Purchases and stock

  • Order from a supplier with quantities, prices, tax and expected delivery.
  • Record each delivery, including damaged or missing goods.
  • Stock changes only when you press Add to stock, so a delivery you have not checked never changes your count.
  • A payment to the supplier is added to Expenses automatically.
  • Print or export an order as PDF or Excel.

Stock is a count per item type, and you choose which types to track. It is not a barcode or per-product inventory system.

Backups and safety

  • Backups locked with a password you choose, made every day, week or month.
  • Share a backup to WhatsApp to keep a copy away from the device, and get a reminder if you have not.
  • Restore on a new computer or phone with the backup file and its password.
  • An owner PIN protects the owner's pages.

A forgotten backup password cannot be recovered, so write it down.

Six languages, your colours

  • English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil and Telugu, in the app and on the printed bill.
  • Several colour themes, light or dark, and bigger text.

How the pieces work together

1. BillOne bill records the sale, who sold it and how it was paid.
2. CommissionThe same bill updates the seller's commission.
3. CreditIf it was on credit, the customer's dues go up.
4. Today and ReportsSales, payments and expenses add up to cash after expenses.
5. PurchasesSupplier payments become expenses; delivered goods go to stock when you say so.
6. BackupA password-protected copy keeps it all safe.

How it works

From download to first bill in minutes

  1. Install

    Download the Windows installer or the Android file below and open it.

  2. Register once

    Enter your shop's details. This needs internet once and starts your 15-day free trial.

  3. Set up your shop

    Add your shop name, item types (garments, dress, saree and so on), your employees and an owner PIN.

  4. Bill

    Type the employee number, the item type number and the price. Choose how the customer pays and save.

  5. Check and back up

    Look at Today and Reports, record expenses, and keep a backup copy away from the device.

  6. Keep going after the trial

    When the 15 days end, open Settings, then Licence, and pay by UPI inside the app.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to register and start the free trial, and for the occasional licence check. Making bills never needs it.

How do I pay?

Inside the app: Settings, then Licence. Choose a plan and pay by UPI. If anything is unclear, message us on WhatsApp.

Can I get a refund?

Ask us within 7 days of paying: message us on WhatsApp.

How does the free trial work?

Every new shop gets 15 days free. After that, a licence key keeps ShopBill running (see Pricing).

Where is my data kept?

On your own computer or phone, in an encrypted database. Bills, customers and prices are never sent to us. Only the registration details, the licence check and, if you switch it on, crash reports leave the device.

What if my computer or phone is lost or breaks?

Restore your latest backup on the new device with its file and password. That is why ShopBill reminds you to keep one backup copy away from the device. A forgotten backup password cannot be recovered.

Does it file my GST or income tax?

No. It keeps the records and prepares the tables and files your accountant asks for. The accountant files the return.

Which devices does it run on?

Windows computers and Android phones and tablets. There is no iPhone, iPad or Mac version.

Which printers work?

Bills are laid out for 58 mm and 80 mm rolls and for A4, and are printed through the printer you choose. If a bill does not print the way you want on your printer, message us and we will help.

Is there stock and inventory?

Yes, as a count per item type that goes down when you sell and goes up when you press Add to stock on a purchase order. There is no barcode or per-product catalogue.

What happens when my subscription ends?

ShopBill keeps working for 7 days with a warning. Then billing stops, but the licence, payment, backup and support screens stay open so you can renew and take your data out.

Can I get a demo?

The 15-day free trial is the demo: install it and try it with your own items. If you would like help getting started, message us on WhatsApp.

How do I get help?

Message us on WhatsApp or send us an email.
